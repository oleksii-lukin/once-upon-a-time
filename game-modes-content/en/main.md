# Main Mode

The classic Once Upon a Time experience. Players take turns as the Storyteller, playing cards that match their narration. Other players can interrupt by playing matching keywords or objecting to cards that don't fit the story.

## Usage Examples

- "Player 1 (Storyteller): 'In a quiet village lived a brave knight who dreamed of adventure...' (plays Knight card)"
- "Player 2: 'Knight! Actually, it was a cowardly merchant who stumbled upon a magic sword!' (interrupts with Knight card, takes over as Storyteller)"
- "Player 2 (new Storyteller): 'The merchant found the sword near an enchanted forest where dragons guarded ancient treasures...' (plays Forest card)"
- "Player 3: 'Forest! But it wasn't enchanted - it was a dark, cursed woodland that swallowed travelers whole!' (interrupts with Forest card)"
