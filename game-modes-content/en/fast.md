# Fast Mode

A quicker version of the game. Interruptions are disabled, allowing the Storyteller to focus on their narrative without constant takeover. Perfect for shorter sessions or learning the flow.

## Usage Examples

- "Player 1 (Storyteller): 'Once upon a time, in a kingdom far away, there lived a young princess with magical powers...' (plays Princess card)"
- "Player 1: 'She discovered an ancient spellbook hidden in the castle library...' (plays Book card)"
- "Player 1: 'The spellbook revealed a prophecy about a dragon that would threaten the realm...' (plays Dragon card)"
- "Player 1: 'Armed with this knowledge, the princess set out to find the legendary sword that could defeat the beast...' (plays Sword card, then plays Ending card to win)"
