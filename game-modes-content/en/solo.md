# Solo Mode

A sandbox for creative writing and practice. You are the only storyteller. No pressure, no competition. Just you and the cards.

## Usage Examples

- "You: 'In the heart of an enchanted forest, a lonely witch tended a garden of magical herbs...' (plays Witch card)"
- "You: 'Her peaceful life changed when a wounded knight stumbled into her clearing, seeking refuge from an evil sorcerer...' (plays Knight card)"
- "You: 'The witch healed the knight, and together they discovered the sorcerer was planning to awaken an ancient dragon...' (plays Dragon card)"
- "You: 'With the help of forest spirits and an enchanted sword, they journeyed to stop the ritual and save the kingdom...' (plays Sword card, then Ending card to complete your story)"
