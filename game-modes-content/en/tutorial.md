# Tutorial Mode

Simplified rules for beginners. Each turn is limited to playing exactly 1 card or exchanging a card for a new one. No interruptions or objections. Helps you learn how to connect your hand to a story.

## Usage Examples

- "Player 1: 'In a peaceful forest, there lived a wise old wizard who could talk to animals...' (plays Wizard card, turn ends)"
- "Player 2: 'The wizard's best friend was a mischievous fairy who loved playing pranks on travelers...' (plays Fairy card, turn ends)"
- "Player 1: 'One day, the fairy discovered a mysterious map that led to a hidden treasure...' (plays Map card, turn ends)"
- "Player 2: 'The map showed the way to an ancient castle where a sleeping princess awaited her true love...' (plays Castle card, turn ends)"
- "Player 1: 'Unable to continue the story with current cards, exchanges one card for a new one and passes the turn.'"
