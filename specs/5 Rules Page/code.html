Core Rules

Learn the fundamentals of how to play 'Once Upon a Time'.

Welcome, Storyteller, to the enchanted world of Once Upon a Time. The goal is to guide the story to your secret ending. This guide will walk you through setting up the game, understanding your turn, and ultimately, how to claim victory by telling the best tale.

Game Objective

expand_more
Each player has a secret "Happily Ever After" card. The first player to play all of their Story cards and guide the narrative to the conclusion on their Ending card wins the game. This requires creativity, strategy, and a little bit of luck.

Game Setup

expand_more
  1. Shuffle the Story cards and deal them out according to the number of players.
  2. Shuffle the Ending cards and deal one to each player, face down. This is their secret ending.
  3. Place the remaining cards in draw piles in the center of the table.
  4. The player who most recently told a story goes first.

Winning & Losing

expand_more
You win the game by playing your final Story card and then playing your Ending card, successfully concluding the tale. There is no traditional "losing," but the story continues until one player achieves their goal.

Quick Reference

Story Cards

Cards used to build the narrative. Include characters, items, and events.

Example: The Dark Forest

Ending Cards

Your secret goal. Concludes the story in a specific way.

Example: They Married

Interrupt Cards

Played to take over storytelling from another player.

Example: A Different Path
